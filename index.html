Research & Thesis

Current focus: Egocentric vision for biker safety analysis

EgoRider: Egocentric Video Analysis for Biker Safety

Research Objective

Develop a comprehensive computer vision pipeline to analyze egocentric (first-person) video data from bikers, enabling systematic study of rider behavior patterns and identification of safety risks. The project aims to bridge the gap between raw video data and actionable safety insights through deep learning and temporal analysis.

Dataset Overview

Self-collected egocentric video dataset captured from real-world biker perspectives. The dataset includes diverse riding scenarios, traffic conditions, and environmental contexts to ensure comprehensive coverage of typical and edge-case behaviors.

  • Source: First-person POV cameras mounted on bikers
  • Content: Real-world riding scenarios across varied conditions
  • Current Status: Data collection and annotation pipeline in development

Methodology & Approach

Status: Model selection in progress

Evaluating two primary architectural directions for temporal action recognition:

CNN + LSTM Approach

Spatial feature extraction using convolutional networks combined with LSTM for temporal sequence modeling

Transformer-Based Approach

Self-attention mechanisms for learning spatial-temporal relationships directly from video frames

Expected Outcomes

  • Safety Insights: Quantifiable metrics on risky behaviors and collision-prone patterns
  • Behavior Analysis Framework: Systematic categorization of rider actions and decision-making patterns
  • Structured Pipeline: Reproducible methodology for egocentric video analysis in transportation safety
  • Academic Contribution: Documentation and potential publication of findings

Responsible Research

This research is conducted with careful attention to privacy, data ethics, and the responsible use of AI in safety-critical applications. All data collection follows ethical guidelines, and the goal is to contribute positively to road safety outcomes.
